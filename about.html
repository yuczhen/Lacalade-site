About Us

﻿Settled in 2014 in Taipei city, La Calade Games comprises various professionals from the gaming industry. We have devoted ourselves to developing causal games, such as Hidden Object Adventure Games and Slot Games, which consists of multiple themes and creative playing experiences. We provide the services with the professional game design and reliable cooperations to worldwide clients. We believe our products can give all players an impressive game experience.

Our services include:
* Game design (including APP and HTML5 Web)
* 2D+3D design
* Math design (for slot games)
* Business cooperation

關於樂卡迪 

樂卡迪遊戲有限公司於2014年9月成立，辦公室設立於台北，成員皆由遊戲相關產業的專業人員所組成，廣納具有寫作、美術及遊戲製作專長人才，希望為所研發的產品注入更優質的創作力。目前主力研發偵探解謎類型的歐美休閒遊戲並同時開發老虎機手遊，也為來自全球客戶提供遊戲開發、美術與APP設計等相關合作。期盼帶給玩家更豐富的遊戲體驗!
